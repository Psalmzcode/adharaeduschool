THE PROBLEM

Schools can't deliver — or prove — practical tech education

  • Theory-heavy ICT; little hands-on coding or digital literacy
  • No trained facilitators or structured curriculum at scale
  • Students use cybercafés for basic digital tasks — not school labs
  • Girls, rural & public-school students left out of bootcamps
  • Admins juggle WhatsApp, Excel, paper registers
  • No verifiable certificates or assessment data for funders
  • One-off NGO workshops don't sustain across terms
  • Result: youth unprepared for jobs & freelancing
